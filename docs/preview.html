Why the way we farm is the real climate question

Practices that were once standard are now under scrutiny. For decades the debate has focused on energy, but a growing number of researchers argue that the sustainability of current farming deserves at least as much attention — soil degradation and biodiversity loss are both accelerating.

Critics say that inadequate regulation has allowed the problem to compound, while the incentives facing farmers still reward short-term yield over long-term resilience.
